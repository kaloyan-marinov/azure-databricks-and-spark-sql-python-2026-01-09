A medallion architecture is a data design pattern used to organize data logically.
Its goal is to
incrementally and progressively improve the structure and quality of data
as it flows through each layer of the architecture.

    bronze                      ->      silver              ->      gold

The recommended data format for each layer is DELTA LAKE
b/c it guarantees
    Atomicity
    Consistency
    Isolation
    Durability
as data passes through each layer.

This layered design makes your platform

    easier to debug         (b/c raw data is always preseved)

    easier to trust         (b/c each layer builds up quality)

    easier to scale         (b/c you can optimize the layers indepedently)

    easier to govern        (with clear separation of responsibility among the layers)

It gives you structure
without locking you into a rigid blueprint.
