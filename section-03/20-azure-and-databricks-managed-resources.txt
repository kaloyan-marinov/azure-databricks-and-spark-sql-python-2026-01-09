03-azure-and-databricks-managed-resources.txt



when you provision a Databricks service,
two things will be provisioned in your Azure account:

    firstly (and obviously),
    the Databricks service itself

    secondly (and not obviously),
    a resource group which is comprised of the following newly-provisioned resources:

        a Virtual Network           (provides the network infrastructure)

        a Network security group    (controls the traffic to and from the workspace)

        an Access Connector for Azure Databricks (facilitates access to other Azure services from within the Databricks environment)

        a Storage Account           (primarily to hold the workspace's managed tables and volumens within Unity Catalog, along with the associated metadata)

        a Managed Identity          (provides an identity for the workspace to access other Azure resources securely)

    thirdly,
    a resource group:

        NetworkWatcherRG            (is automatically provisioned by Azure)
