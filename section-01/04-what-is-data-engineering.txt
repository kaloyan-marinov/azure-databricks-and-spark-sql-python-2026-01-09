Data Engineering is
the practice of designing, building, and operationg systems
that turn raw data into reliable, analysis-ready information.



Every data pipeline follows a common flow:

    1 ingestion
    2 staging
    3 transform
    4 serve



Two foundational Data Integration Patterns follow these pipelines:

    Extract, Transform and Load (ETL)

    Extract, Load and Transform (ELT)



    Please note:
    Although Databricks embraces ETL and ELT,
    the documentation uses ETL as a familiar umbrella term.



Data Processing can run in 2 modes:

    batch

        handles chunks of data

        fixed schedule

        ideal for aggregation and bulk updates

    stream

        continuously processes data as it arrives

        real-time analysis



Robust data engineering ensures that
analysis, data scientists, and applications receive fresh, accurate data
without spending hours fixing broken pipelines.
