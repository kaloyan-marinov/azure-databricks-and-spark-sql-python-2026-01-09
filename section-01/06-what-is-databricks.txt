06-what-is-databricks.txt



Databricks is a unified, cloud-native Data Intelligence Platform.

It combines
    data ingestion,
    storage,
    processing,
    analytics,
    and even machine learning
- so you don't have to stitch together multiple disconnected tools.




Under the hood,
Databricks runs on Apache Spark
[which is] the industry-standard engine for large-scale data processing.
Spark gives you fast parallel execution for both batch and streaming workloads.



Databricks ... works seamlessly with each cloud's core services.



At its heart sits the Data Lakehouse.



Databricks provides 5 core engines to turn your data into value:

    business intelligence       (build live dashboards and run reports)

    data warehousing            (run high-performance, large-scale SQL queries)

    data science and AI         (explore data interactively + built-in experiment tracking and model management via MLFlow)

    streaming                   (ETL and real-time analytics; create both batch ETL pipelines and streaming jobs in one platform)

    orchestration               (automate end-to-end workflows)



That's Databricks in a nutshell:
a unified, cloud-native lakehouse powered by Spark
that streamlines every stage of the data lifecycle.
